## Doubts (DUBBI)

**5. Adjunct faculty 2023 "treated as missing" is only a label.**
No regression, frontier or table uses adjunct faculty, and every exposure uses 2020-2022, so the
2023 zeros enter no estimate. But nothing recodes them: in project_panel (05) and in the 06 sample
the 2023 values are still 0; table_adjunct_check.csv only says "treated as missing".
*Proposed fix:* in our code, write a group copy of the project panel with adjunct 2023 set to NA,
check that only those 61 cells change, and verify that all estimates are unchanged.

**7. Extremes of the student/faculty ratio (13.8 and 58.0).**
Minimum: Perugia Stranieri 2022, 955 / 69 = 13.84 (faculty 55 → 69, +25% in one year; students
1,106 → 955). Maximum: Bergamo 2020, 21,746 / 375 = 57.99 (falls to 40.4 in 2023 as faculty grows
to 492). Both look plausible (very small specialised university; fast-growing large one), but the
Perugia Stranieri jump is a large within-university change in the exposure.
*Proposed fix:* add a data note and verify the 2022 faculty count of Perugia Stranieri at the source.

**9. "More students at fixed faculty" in 09 has no tolerance, and the "4 cases" are not fixed faculty.**
120 changes = 60 universities × 2 (2020-21, 2021-22); the ratio rises 17 times (8 both up, 4 both
down, 4 students up / faculty down, 1 students up / faculty unchanged). The direction uses the exact
sign of dlog. The 4 cases are students up and faculty *down* (dlog F from −1.4% to −3.2%); only 1
case has faculty exactly unchanged (Napoli Parthenope 2020-21). Students up with |dlog F| ≤ 1%: 4;
≤ 2%: 9.
*Proposed fix:* explicit definition in 09 with a declared band (main ±1%, sensitivity exact and ±2%),
in a separate table with a note.

**12 (note). NHN_Z: which solution the cluster SE refers to.**
The cluster SE 0.537 is computed for the re-estimate (δ1 = 0.491, log-likelihood 44.856), not for
the kit fit (δ1 = 0.416, log-likelihood 44.742, convergence code 0 but below the maximum).
*Proposed fix:* report both, each with its log-likelihood and cluster SE; cite the higher likelihood.

**16. ap outlier rule is mechanical and declared only in a code comment.**
13 university-years (7 universities: Roma La Sapienza ×3, Napoli Federico II ×3, Bologna 2022-23,
Milano 2021 and 2023, Padova 2023, Torino 2023, Verona 2023) are flagged because every observation
that appears in any of the 12 deletion sets of ap (NDEL = 12) is flagged; by construction at least 12
are flagged. ap.plot shows clear separation only at 1 deletion (Roma La Sapienza 2021) and at 4
(adding Bologna 2023, Torino 2023, Roma La Sapienza 2022). Impact is nil: R6 Spearman 1.000.
The diary calls them "all LARGE"; Verona (838 faculty, 2,701 publications) is mid-sized.
*Proposed fix:* write the rule and the gaps in table_12_notes.csv; correct the diary.

**17. Subsampling intervals lie above the estimate; some subsample sizes are very small.**
nrep = 1000, seed 20261122, subsample.sizes = NULL (36 candidates m = 2-37; chosen m at 95%:
median 25, range 2-35). Median 95% width 0.0709 (relative 4.7%): confirmed. In all 183 cases the
lower bound is ≥ the DEA estimate (equal in 32). This is expected: the intervals are for the true
Farrell score; the DEA estimate is biased towards efficiency (λ̂ ≤ λ in the output orientation), so
the intervals already correct for the bias while the point estimate does not. In the figures the
segment therefore lies on one side of the point, which is not explained. Cassino 2021 has m = 2;
45 observations have m < 10.
*Proposed fix:* note in the table and under the figure; flag intervals with m < 10.

**18. Plausibility of the 3 efficient observations.**
Catanzaro 2021 (highest publications per faculty, 4.83; medicine share 43%), Bari Politecnico 2022
(4.03; engineering share 62%), Molise 2021 (highest publications per million EUR, 96.8). The frontier
does not adjust for field composition, and fields with high Scopus output per author can define the
frontier. Molise has the lowest current-management cost per faculty member in 2020-2022 (27k EUR,
median 67k), current-management costs of 7.9 M EUR in 2020 against operating costs of 44.3 M EUR,
and a +38% jump in 2022.
*Proposed fix:* compare Molise's cost items with similar universities (report only, no data change);
discuss field composition in the report.

**22. Figures of 15.**
Grey scale only, distinct symbols and line types, no red/green pairs; titles are descriptive (only the
DAG uses causal wording, by design). Minor defects: in fig_15_efficiency_vs_load the bottom-left
legend covers data points (around 0.19 and 0.07); in fig_15_university_quadrants the subtitle touches
the title. fig_12_efficiency_vs_load has the same bottom-left legend.
*Proposed fix:* move the legends off the data; separate title and subtitle.

**Other (code robustness).** 13 writes "DEA CRS" by hand for the main row, and 15 hard-codes it in a
check and in the figure title. If the open sample decision changes the estimator of 12, the labels
would be wrong without any error. *Proposed fix:* read the estimator from the outputs of 12.

**Other (bootstrap test).** NREP = 200 triggers FEAR's warning ("may cause unreliable results").
*Proposed fix:* time one run with NREP = 1000; adopt it under RUN_SENSITIVITY if it is fast enough.

**Other (log).** The warning "NHN_Z still reports non-convergence" printed during the run comes from
the instructor's 02_frontiers.R (common exercise), not from our scripts.

## Problems (PROBLEMI)

**13. Faculty and cost elasticities 0.70 / 0.32 come from the unidentified NHN.**
They are the frontier coefficients of the 06 NHN with the load in the frontier (0.696 and 0.317),
whose Hessian is not positive definite (minimum eigenvalue −0.0076) and whose likelihood drifts to
σv → 0 (item 11). They must not be cited as results. They do not currently appear in CLAUDE.md, the
diary, the README or the note for the professor. Alternatives: NHN_Z (Hessian positive definite)
0.717 (cluster SE 0.094) and 0.302 (cluster SE 0.072), a pooled model without university effects;
OLS with university and year effects, column (6): 0.380 (0.368) and −0.054 (0.126).
*Proposed fix:* never cite the NHN elasticities; where returns to scale are discussed, use NHN_Z with
cluster SEs and say that it is a maintained pooled specification.

  mid-sized.

*Proposed fix:* update CLAUDE.md and the diary; use the corrected wording in the report.
